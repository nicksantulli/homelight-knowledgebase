# BBYS Competitors — Homeward, Opendoor & Knock

> Reference for HomeLight BBYS positioning vs. Homeward, Opendoor, and Knock. Claude reads this for competitive comp work, LO talk tracks, and strategic planning. Full deliverables: BBYS-Competitive-Analysis-Homeward-Opendoor.docx (Word), BBYS-Competitive-Analysis-Deck.pptx (PPT), BBYS-Competitive-Battlecard.html / .pdf (battlecard), BBYS-Competitive-Sources-Confidence.md (source log) — all in the vault root.

## The fundamental model differences

The three products are **not** the same thing. This is the most important framing:

| Player | Model | What the customer experiences |
|--------|-------|------------------------------|
| **HomeLight** | Equity-unlock LOAN against the departing residence (DR) | Customer keeps title to both homes throughout. Lists DR on open market. |
| **Homeward** | Sale-leaseback iBuyer | Homeward buys new home with cash; customer rents from Homeward; customer buys it back after DR sells. Two transactions. |
| **Opendoor** | Cash buyer of old home — **no true BBYS** | Sells old home to Opendoor; uses cash to be non-contingent on new purchase. No equity bridge. |
| **Knock** | Bridge LOAN against the departing residence (DR), capped at **75% CLTV** | Customer keeps title to both homes and lists DR on the open market (same shape as HomeLight). Two flavors: leave the existing mortgage in place, or pay it off. |

## Homeward — key facts (HIGH confidence from homeward.com)

- **Equity Unlock**: ~80% of DR market value. Approval in ≤24 hours.
- **Fees**: 3.5% standard / **2.5% if customer uses Homeward Mortgage** (1% credit). Covers first 90 days.
- **Extensions**: 1%/month for up to 3 additional months (180-day cap). New construction: up to 360 days, no extra fee.
- **Floor Price guarantee**: If DR doesn't sell in 180 days, Homeward buys it at Equity Unlock amount (typically 90–95% of market value minus 6% commission). Homeward eats any resale loss; passes any gain back.
- **States (DR location)**: TX, FL, GA, NC, SC, TN, WA, OR, AZ, CO, DC — **11 jurisdictions only**. New home can be anywhere in the US.
- **New-home loan compatibility**: Conventional, Jumbo, VA, Land — **NOT FHA or USDA**.
- **Channel**: **Approved Lender program** — outside LO originates the new-home mortgage. Equity Unlock loan itself is **locked to Homeward Mortgage**. In FL/WA/OR, the entire product is offered by Homeward Mortgage.
- **Customer must list DR within 2 weeks** of new-home close. Pays prorated carrying costs (taxes, utilities, lawn) during rent period. Final appraisal required at buyback.

## Opendoor — key facts (HIGH confidence from opendoor.com + industry)

- **No true BBYS product**. Their answer is a cash offer that converts the customer into a non-contingent buyer.
- **Cash Now, More Later** (formerly Cash Plus, renamed 3/10/2026): Opendoor buys + renovates + resells, customer gets upfront cash plus share of upside, no clawback on downside. Closes in 14–60 days.
- **Service fee**: ~5% on the cash offer (industry-reported).
- **Mortgage** (beta, launched Feb 2026): **4.99% 30-year fixed conventional**, no points/fees. Limited to buyers of **Opendoor-owned homes in Denver + Colorado Springs only**.
- **How Opendoor funds the rate**: Explicitly by **removing loan officer commissions** (~350bps in industry savings). CEO Kaz Nejatian said it's "not a forever deal."
- **Acquired HomeBuyer.com** in Dec 2025 to power the mortgage relaunch.
- **Channels**:
  - **Key Agent™ Program** — tiered Silver / Gold / Platinum, agents earn 1% commission
  - **Key Connections** — Feb 2025 pilot in 16 markets, expanding nationally. Sellers paired with embedded agent partners
  - Lennar builder partnership; Opendoor Exclusives marketplace (Austin, Houston, DFW)
- **Mortgage division was shut down Nov 2022**, relaunched 2026. Reported $1.3B net loss in 2025.

## Knock — key facts (MEDIUM-HIGH confidence; mechanics from an LO/Account-Manager email with a real worked scenario, 2026-06-04)

Knock's BBYS answer is a **bridge loan** against the departing residence — the same fundamental model as HomeLight (customer keeps title to both homes, lists the DR on the open market), **not** an iBuyer sale-leaseback like Homeward. The differentiators are the math: **lower CLTV cap (75%)**, a **2.25% contract fee charged in every case**, and two program variants.

### Shared inputs (how Knock sizes the loan)
- **Value basis** = the lower of (a) the realtor's estimated list price and (b) Knock's internal AVM, which they call the **"Predicted Sales Price."** The AVM is **subject to review by Knock's Risk Analysis team and may change.**
- **Max CLTV = 75%** of that value basis. This is the **lowest CLTV cap in the competitive set** (HomeLight up to 90% with HELOC Equity Boost; Homeward ~80%).
- **Contract fee = 2.25% of the estimated list price** — charged in **both** programs.
- **Closing costs ≈ $1,850** (approximate, both programs).

### Two programs

**Option 1 — Knock Bridge Loan** (leaves the existing mortgage in place; gives the borrower 6 months of PITI)
- **Max Total Bridge Loan = (75% × value) − existing mortgage balance.** Because the existing mortgage is left in place, the bridge only captures the thin equity sliver *above* the current loan, so this number is small (and can go negative — see worked example).
- Deductions from that bridge to reach usable funds:
  - **6 months of PITI** on the departing residence — set aside and disbursed *after* closing
  - **Contract fee** = 2.25% of estimated list price
  - **~$1,850** closing costs
- Borrower **keeps their existing (often low-rate) mortgage**, but usable funds are frequently negative in high-balance / low-equity situations.

**Option 2 — Knock Bridge Loan Plus** (pays off the existing mortgage)
- **Max Total Bridge Loan = 75% × value** (full CLTV — no subtraction, because the existing mortgage is being paid off out of the proceeds).
- Deductions:
  - **Contract fee** = 2.25% of estimated list price
  - **Existing 1st-mortgage payoff** (full balance)
  - **Origination fee = 1% of the total loan amount** (this 1% fee is **unique to the Plus program**)
  - **~$1,850** closing costs
- Net = funds available **toward the down payment on the new home**. Unlocks far more cash than Option 1, but the borrower **loses their existing mortgage rate** and eats an extra 1% origination fee.

### Worked example (from the forwarded email — DR value $557,947)
Inputs: Predicted Sales Price (AVM) **$557,947**; Max CLTV 75% → **$418,460**; existing mortgage balance **~$393,076.78**; list price was TBD so 2.25% fee was run off the AVM.

| | **Option 1 — Bridge Loan** | **Option 2 — Bridge Loan Plus** |
|---|---|---|
| Max Total Bridge Loan | $25,383  *(418,460 − 393,077)* | $418,460  *(full 75% CLTV)* |
| − 6 months PITI (set aside) | −$16,754.64  *(≈$2,792/mo)* | — |
| − Contract fee (2.25% of list) | −$12,553.81 | −$12,553.81 |
| − Existing mortgage payoff | — | −$393,076.78 |
| − Plus origination fee (1% of loan) | — | −$4,184.60 |
| − Closing costs | −$1,850 | −$1,850 |
| **= Net to customer** | **−$9,959.45**  *(negative — deal doesn't pencil)* | **+$6,794.81**  *(toward new-home down payment)* |

Same borrower, same home: Option 1 produces *nothing* (the 75% cap minus their existing balance leaves almost no room, and the 6-month PITI reserve + fees push it negative), while Option 2 frees ~$6.8K by paying off the mortgage — at the cost of the borrower's existing rate plus a 1% origination fee.

### Talk-track takeaways vs. Knock
- **CLTV gap is the headline**: Knock caps at **75%**. HomeLight reaches **up to 90% CLTV** with HELOC Equity Boost — materially more equity unlocked on the same home.
- **Knock's "leave the mortgage" option rarely works**: at 75% CLTV minus the existing balance, Option 1 nets little or nothing for any borrower with a normal LTV. The only way Knock unlocks real cash is Option 2, which forces a **full payoff** (kills the low-rate mortgage) **and** adds a **1% origination fee** on top of the 2.25% contract fee.
- **Fee stack**: Knock = 2.25% contract fee (always) + 1% origination (Plus only). Compare to Homeward's clean 2.5%-with-their-mortgage and HomeLight's program fee.
- **AVM risk**: Knock's loan size hangs on an internal "Predicted Sales Price" that their Risk team can revise downward — an uncertainty to surface to borrowers.

## HomeLight strengths vs. each (talk tracks)

### vs. Homeward
- **Equity unlock**: HomeLight up to **90% CLTV with HELOC Equity Boost** (launched 3/4/2026) vs. Homeward's ~80%
- **Customer experience**: HomeLight customer **never gives up title, never rents, never faces a buyback final appraisal**. Homeward requires both
- **Loan-type compatibility**: HomeLight supports Conv + FHA + VA; Homeward excludes FHA + USDA
- **Geographic breadth**: HomeLight operates in many more markets — Homeward DR location is limited to 11 jurisdictions
- **Extension flexibility**: HomeLight's 180/240/300-day extension stack + Jake Vogel's exception authority beats Homeward's 90-day-base / 180-day-cap

### vs. Opendoor
- **Opendoor isn't BBYS**: customer sells DR to Opendoor at wholesale price, gives up open-market upside, still has to qualify for the new mortgage on their own. HomeLight unlocks equity AND lets the customer list on the open market for full appreciation
- **LO economics**: With HomeLight, the LO originates the new-home loan. With Opendoor's 4.99% mortgage, LO commissions are explicitly removed

### vs. Knock
- **Equity unlock**: HomeLight up to **90% CLTV** (HELOC Equity Boost) vs. Knock's **75%** — the widest CLTV gap of any competitor in this file
- **Keep-your-mortgage trap**: Knock's Option 1 (leave the existing mortgage) structurally nets almost nothing because the 75% cap minus the existing balance leaves no room. HomeLight unlocks equity without forcing the borrower to choose between "keep my rate but get no cash" and "pay off my low-rate mortgage"
- **Fee story**: Knock charges 2.25% in every case **plus** a 1% origination fee on the Plus program; the borrower also loses their existing mortgage rate to get real cash. Position HomeLight's program fee and rate-preservation against that stack
- **Same fundamental model**: unlike Homeward/Opendoor, Knock IS a bridge loan with the customer keeping title and listing on the open market — so the win is on **CLTV, fees, and not having to kill the existing mortgage**, not on the model shape

## HomeLight competitive vulnerabilities (what to watch)

- **Fee transparency**: Homeward's published 2.5% (with Homeward Mortgage) is structurally clean. HomeLight's variable program fee + Jake Vogel exception path is harder to communicate upfront. A public-style fee sheet would help LO sales motion
- **Floor-price narrative**: Homeward's "we will buy your home" pitch is clearer than the DTI Drop story. Reposition DTI Drop as a floor-price equivalent
- **Speed to approval**: Homeward publicly commits to ≤24-hour Equity Unlock approval. HomeLight has 120 apps stuck at "submitted" — speed is both a sales differentiator and an ops target
- **HELOC EB state gaps**: HELOC Equity Boost not available in TX, NY, AK, MA, VA, VT, IA — partial offset to the 90% pitch
- **HELOC EB conventional-only**: FHA/VA borrowers can't reach 90% CLTV
- **Opendoor's 4.99% mortgage trajectory**: Even constrained today, the strategic intent (remove LO commissions to fund rate) is the long-term existential pressure on HomeLight's LO channel
