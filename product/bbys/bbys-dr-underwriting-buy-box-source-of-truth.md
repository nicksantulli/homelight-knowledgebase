# BBYS DR Underwriting Buy Box — Source of Truth

**INTERNAL USE ONLY** · Last updated **2026-07-01** · Document owner: **Jason Smith**

> Canonical underwriting buy-box rules for Departure Residence (DR) eligibility. Supersedes informal tribal knowledge; pair with [[2026-07-01-florida-bbys-full-reopening]] for Florida-specific CLTV and geography.

## Max Combined Loan to Value (CLTV)

| Product / valuation basis | Max CLTV |
|---|---|
| Equity Unlock (EU) — HL valuation | **85%** |
| Asset Equity Boost — greater of HL or HouseCanary valuation | **85%** |
| HELOC Boost — LO DR valuation | **90%** |

**Special limits:** Florida and the City of Chicago, IL — see Geographic Areas below and [[2026-07-01-florida-bbys-full-reopening]].

## Financing Guidelines

- Conventional loans are eligible.
- FHA/VA loans are eligible **only if** using a conventional loan for IR.
- HELOC with balance must be paid off with Equity Unlock.
- Second mortgages must be paid off with Equity Unlock.
- Reverse mortgages may be considered with approval of the Director of Real Estate Operations and President of HomeLight Homes (HLH).
- Client credit score minimum: **620**.

## Equity Unlock Amount

- Unlock up to **$2M** in EU.
- Maximum Loan Payoff Value (LPV) for any BBYS loan: **$2M**.
- Any LPV in excess of $2M requires approval from the President of HLH and Director of Real Estate Operations before conditional approval.

## Eligible Property Types

- Single-family homes
- 1–4 unit properties (all units vacant at funding)
- Townhomes
- Properties with annualized HOA fees **< 1%** of property value
- Condominiums in buildings with **< 5 stories**
- Properties valued at **$150,000+**

## Property Characteristics

| Rule | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Square footage (above grade) | 750–5,500 SF (outside bounds → Director of RE Ops approval) |
| SFR bedrooms/baths | Min 2 bed / 1 bath |
| Condo/townhome bedrooms/baths | Min 1 bed / 1 bath (no studios) |
| Vacancy | Must be vacant of tenants from agreement signed onward |
| Lot size | ≤ 5 acres eligible; > 5 acres → Director of RE Ops approval |

### Condo Eligibility

- Min 1 bed / 1 bath (no studios)
- Hotel-style condos: **ineligible**
- FHA/VA non-warrantable condos: **ineligible**

## Listing & Days on Market

- Listed **150+ days** on market (including under contract, accepting backups, contingent, or pending) in the last 365 days: **ineligible**
- For Sale By Owner (FSBO) listings: **ineligible**

## Ineligible Property Types

- Fewer than 3 similar comps within a reasonable radius (per Approval Team)
- Greater than 4 units
- Log, A-frame, geodesic dome, or "Barndominum" style homes
- Co-op ownership
- 55+ community
- Tenancy in common
- Mixed use
- Part of an active new construction community
- Mobile / manufactured / modular homes (including permanent foundations)
- Active foreclosures, short sales, or bankruptcies
- HOA with pending litigation
- Property value < $150,000
- Vacant land
- Properties with adjacent vacant parcels (client may only use BBYS on the parcel the home sits on; adjacent parcels must be listed/sold separately and are not subject to backup offer)
- Ineligible if access is only through a separately-sold vacant parcel
- If structure sits on two parcels, both parcels are eligible

## Requires Director of RE Ops Exception

- High-rise condos or condos in buildings with 5+ stories
- Annualized HOA fees > 1% of property value
- Property subject to any leases, including tenancy, oil/gas/mineral rights

## Property Ownership & Deed Restrictions

- Leaseholds: **ineligible**
- Outstanding life estates: **ineligible**
- Deed restrictions: generally **ineligible**
- Trusts: trust agreement required before approval
- Second homes / investment properties: may be considered; borrower must qualify for both mortgages (no DTI removal); equity loan only
- **Solar:** any lien from solar financing/leasing must be fully paid; solar lease need not be paid off but must be in econ model with **min $10k holdback** from EU

## Access, Utilities & Zoning

- Unencumbered access to public roads (no easements through other lots)
- Access to utilities (water, electric)
- Zoned residential
- Wells: well equipment inspection + water quality test before final approval

## Renovations, Repairs & Inspections

- No ongoing renovations affecting bathroom, kitchen, drywall, or landscaping
- No major defects or ongoing repairs/upgrades to foundation, framing, roof, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, fireplace, chimney
- Inspection required on homes **> 5 years old** and every home in a federally-declared disaster area (FEMA)

## Geographic Areas with Special Considerations

- Properties adjacent to an inland channel seawall: **ineligible**
- FEMA Special Flood Hazard Area (Zones A, AE, A1–A30, AH, AO, AR, A99, V, VE, V1–V30): **ineligible**
- Known active sinkhole activity areas: **ineligible**
- Galveston, TX: **ineligible**
- City of Chicago, IL: **70% CLTV** limit (HL internal valuation)

### Florida (summary)

- **Only single-family detached** homes eligible (no townhomes, condos, or other types)
- Properties within **2 miles** of Gulf or Atlantic coast: **ineligible**
- Region-specific CLTV limits (75% / 70%): see [[2026-07-01-florida-bbys-full-reopening]]

## BBYS Eligible States

Alabama, Arizona, Arkansas, California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, **Florida** (special limits), Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, **Illinois** (Chicago 70% CLTV), Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New Mexico, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Oregon, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Virginia, Vermont, Washington, Washington D.C., West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming

## BBYS Ineligible States (DTI Drop Only)

Alaska, New York, Massachusetts
