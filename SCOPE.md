# Scope: What Belongs Here

This document defines what content should and should not live in the HomeLight Sales-Team Knowledge Base.

## In Scope (INCLUDE)

### Product Information
- **BBYS (Buy Before You Sell)** — program mechanics, eligibility, pricing, timelines, common scenarios
- **DTI Drop** — how it works, lender requirements, use cases, integration with BBYS
- **HELOC** — HomeLight HELOC product details, rates, eligibility, positioning vs competitors
- **Other Homes Programs** — Cash Offer, Trade-In, Simple Sale, and related offerings
- Program comparison guides and decision trees

### Sales Process & Playbooks
- Sales stages and milestone definitions
- Talk tracks and scripts
- Objection handling guides
- Qualification criteria and discovery questions
- Pricing discussions and fee explanations
- Competitive positioning and differentiation

### Partner & Integration Context
- Lender partner information relevant to sales conversations
- Agent partner context that helps close deals
- Integration touchpoints (how programs connect)
- Partner-specific requirements or constraints that affect sales

### Operations Context (Sales-Relevant)
- Ops processes that sales needs to understand or explain
- Handoff procedures between sales and ops
- Timeline expectations and SLAs
- Escalation paths

### Reference Material
- Glossary of terms and acronyms
- Quick-reference cards and cheat sheets
- FAQ content

---

## Out of Scope (EXCLUDE)

### Meeting Notes & Transient Content
- 1:1 notes, standup notes, retro notes
- Call recordings or transcripts (raw)
- Slack thread exports

### Sensitive & Protected Information
- PII (names, addresses, SSNs, financial details of real customers)
- Secrets, API keys, credentials
- Compensation structures or commission details
- Legal agreements or contracts (full text)
- HR or personnel information

### Internal Technical Systems
- Data Bridge internals, architecture, or implementation details
- Engineering documentation not relevant to sales
- Infrastructure or DevOps content

### Personal Content
- Personal vault dumps or unfiltered Obsidian exports
- Draft content not ready for team use
- Individual task lists or to-dos

### Redundant or Deprecated
- Content that duplicates official sources (link instead)
- Outdated policies or sunset programs (archive separately)

---

## Gray Areas — Ask First

If you're unsure whether content belongs:

1. **Does it help a salesperson do their job?** → Likely in scope
2. **Could it embarrass HomeLight or harm a customer if leaked?** → Out of scope
3. **Is it already well-documented elsewhere?** → Link, don't duplicate
4. **Is it one person's opinion vs. team knowledge?** → Probably out of scope until validated
