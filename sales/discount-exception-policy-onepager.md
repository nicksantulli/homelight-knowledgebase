# Discount & Exception Policy — One-Pager (for sign-off)

**Why:** Discounting today is scattered and exception authority lives in one
person's head. As Builder/UWM scale, that's where margin leaks. This formalizes
the rules and pushes routine approvals down so Jake isn't the bottleneck.

## What's already true (no decision needed)
- **Agent-sourced deals:** 25% comp discount (automatic, property-driven).
- **Client/D2C deals:** $0 comp (automatic).
- **UWM conflict:** 50/50 split on the first 3 conflicted deals; AE relationship
  trumps existing LO assignment.
- **Promos** (e.g., 50 bps) must be time-boxed with start/end dates + tracked segment.

## What we need you to decide

**1. Approval thresholds** — who can approve what size discount?
| Discount off standard | Proposed approver |
|--|--|
| Small (up to ___%/___bps) | LSM |
| Medium (___%–___%) | Jake |
| Large / below margin floor | Friedman |

**2. Margin floor** — the effective-fee or dollar floor below which any discount
escalates to GM sign-off: **$______ / ______%**.

**3. LO-tier exceptions** — do any standing per-LO or per-partner exception deals
exist today that we should grandfather? (None currently documented.)

## What happens after sign-off
- The numbers get filled into the full policy ([[2026-06-22-discount-exception-policy]]).
- Wave 4's next tickets build the **approval workflow** (Slack + Helper) and the
  **discount-leakage report** against this ratified policy.
